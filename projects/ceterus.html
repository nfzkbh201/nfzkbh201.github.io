Ceterus

A workspace for economics questions that refuse to stay tidy. Ceterus turns a messy prompt into hypotheses, paper trails, model notes, critiques, and memos without losing the line back to the original question.

How it works

  • AskStart with a research question, not a rigid form.
  • GatherPull papers, notes, definitions, and competing arguments into one board.
  • Stress testHave Nash critique the model and flag weak assumptions before the memo gets too confident.
  • WriteTurn the evidence trail into a readable research memo with caveats intact.

Why it matters

Research gets chaotic fast: tabs, PDFs, half-finished notes, and beautiful ideas hiding inside terrible file names. Ceterus is the attempt to make the workflow feel alive without making it feel loose.
