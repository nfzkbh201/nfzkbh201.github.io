What it untangles

  • ReportingRepeatable monthly packs instead of hand-built workbook archaeology.
  • Capital callsGenerated notices and summaries with the source files close by.
  • TraceabilityEvery output points back to where the numbers came from.
  • RerunsThe workflow can be corrected and rerun without rebuilding everything manually.

Why it matters

Finance work should be careful, not theatrical. This project is about making the boring parts reliable enough that the human work can move back to judgment, review, and decision-making.
